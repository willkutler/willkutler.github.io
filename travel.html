Travel

The wedding is in Greenville, New York, tucked into the northern edge of the Catskills. It is an easy place to reach by car, and the drive itself is a lovely one once you are out of the city.

If you are coming from New York City, Greenville is roughly a two and a half hour drive north. If you would rather fly, Albany is the closest airport and leaves you about forty minutes from the venue by car.

We would recommend renting a car either way, as there is not much in the way of public transport once you are up here and you will want a way to get between the events over the weekend.
