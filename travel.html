We are so grateful to those traveling to celebrate with us!

Transportation

The closest airport is Albany, which is about an hour drive. LaGuardia and Newark are both 2.5 - 3 hours away. The closest Amtrak station is about 30 minutes away in Hudson.

We would recommend renting a car for the weekend, as there is little public transportation and uber service in the Catskills.

Lodging

We will share information on hotels and booking closer to the date. There will be shuttle service provided to the venue.
